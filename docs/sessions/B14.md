## Design notes

- A public projection is safe by construction, not by filtering: the
  row schema has no field for an owner, an instance, a wallet other than
  the publisher, a draft or an unpublished version, and the store query
  joins only public rows. The test still asserts the absence of every
  private identifier in the responses.
- One leaderboard: the explorer's `performance` is the B13 ranking entry
  for the version, so the same eligibility rules, reasons and order apply
  and a rank in a list is the rank on `/v1/rankings/model`. Sorting by
  followers is offered as a labelled popularity order, never as a rank.
- Followers are offered a version only when they could read and pin it:
  at registration, not at freeze. Nothing a creator or an operator does
  moves a pin; a hidden version's proposals are withdrawn because the
  offer would point at something the follower can no longer read.
- Moderation is a listing decision with a recorded reason, separate from
  the chain: the record stays served, the owner still reads their
  version, publication of a hidden version is refused (B08 rule kept).
- Archiving hides from discovery and the ranking without destroying
  evidence: version pages, series, records and instances stay readable.
- The explorer is computed on read like the rankings (population bounded
  at 500 versions) and rate limited at 60/min per client; a snapshot job
  remains OD-23 territory.

## Unresolved risks and dependencies

- The explorer is computed on read over a population bounded at 500
  registered versions; a persisted snapshot of the ranking and the rows
  is needed before the public population grows (with OD-23).
- The creator identity is the publishing wallet only; a person with two
  verified wallets appears as two creators, and a wallet reused by two
  people appears as one. Any richer creator identity needs a decision
  (who asserts it, how it is verified) before it exists.
- Moderation staffing, review process and appeal path in production are
  open (OD-20 keeps that part).
- Follower notifications (a proposal exists but nobody is told) arrive
  with B16.
- The web explorer, creator pages and moderation-aware version pages are
  F12.
