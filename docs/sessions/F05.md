## Contract gaps and decisions

- Watchlists are now a backend contract (versioned, owner-scoped); notes
  are stored but not yet editable in the app.
- The catalog carries no image URLs; the app draws a monogram and will
  keep doing so until a contract with provenance for issuer imagery exists.
- "Add to basket", "Buy" and "Sell" are listed as not available with the
  session that brings them (F07, F08) and every availability or capability
  reason; no control pretends to trade.
- Price history: no series is stored, so no chart is drawn ("History
  unavailable"); B13 owns the methodology.

## Risks and follow-ups

- Live issuer feeds and real mints remain BLOCKED (OD-17, OD-18); the
  first environment with access must run `configured_url` ingestion and
  re-check the Explore rows against the issuer's own pages.
- The `Button` primitive remounts its element when a disabled reason
  appears or disappears (a wrapper span is added); focus can be lost in
  that moment. Worth changing in the design system (F01 follow-up).
- Search matches symbol prefix, name or company only; documented aliases
  (build prompt) need a backend alias field before they can be searched.
