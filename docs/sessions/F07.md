  eligibility, readiness list, "Review investment" unavailable with its
  reason until F09/F10). Autosave with `ifRevision`; Saving / Saved as
  revision N / Offline changes (device copy with retry) / Conflict (both
  revisions compared; keep mine or take the server's) / Not saved; leaving
  with unsaved edits asks; archive and restore make the draft read-only.
- Docs: frontend README, routes and journeys, contract map, verification,
  security and privacy, README; F06 evidence and journey text updated for
