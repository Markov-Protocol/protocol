## Provider verification status

`execution.jupiter.quote` is FIXTURE_VERIFIED: plans are built from the
synthetic fixture venue (and the configured-URL adapter is exercised
against an in-memory gateway), never from Jupiter. Every Jupiter host was
unreachable from this environment (SR-JUP-01), so the current quote and
swap interface, its credentials, supported mints and route programs are
unverified and the route/program matrix lists no live program; a live
plan therefore fails closed. `execution.jupiter.build` stays DISABLED.
Nothing was signed, submitted, reserved or spent; no message was sent.

## Unresolved risks and dependencies

- The live venue integration (OD-21) is the gate before any plan outside
  fixtures: read the current documentation, map the real response onto
  the quote contract, take and record one live quote for an admitted
  mint, review the route programs into the matrix with evidence.
- Policy decisions are taken at plan time at stage `quote` without a
  reservation; B10 must re-evaluate at stage `submit` and reserve before
  anything is signed, and refuse a plan whose decisions or quotes expired.
- Multi-constituent plans are staged by construction until B11 composes
  and simulates whole-basket transactions; no simulation exists in B09
  (`validity.simulation` is null).
- The fixture venue's prices and route are synthetic; nothing about
  liquidity, price impact or route minimums of a real venue is known.
- Rate limits on plan builds are per client address (20/min) and depend
  on `API_TRUST_PROXY` behind a load balancer.
- Fixture reference marks re-anchor to the hour; two fixture ingestions
  straddling an hour boundary differ by that hour (an "updated" instead
  of "unchanged" outcome), which only tests running across the boundary
  would notice.
