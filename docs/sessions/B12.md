## Design notes

- Quantities only: nothing in B12 values a holding; valuation, performance
  and rankings are B13. USDC and stock mints never share a balancing
  equation.
- Corrections reverse and replace; nothing is updated in place. Duplicate
  observations are one entry by construction (unique source reference per
  owner). External-flow references carry the checkpoint id, the slot and
  the asset, so two observations at the same slot with different
  differences are two entries; the startup check caught the earlier
  slot-only reference silently dropping the second flow.
- A token counts towards one instance only; when tokens leave the wallet
  outside the platform the wallet-level remainder goes negative in the
  holdings answer instead of a strategy's lots being reduced silently.
- Receipts: the signature attests to the record; settlement is the chain
  evidence the record references; the body says so (`scope`). The signed
  body carries commitments for the owner and the actor, so a public read
  redacts the answer without breaking the signature.
- "Latest" checkpoint and receipt listings use an insertion sequence, not a
  timestamp, because timestamps tie within a burst (and under the test
  clock).

## Unresolved risks and dependencies

- Reconciliation attributes every unexplained difference to an external
  flow the owner explains. Token-2022 transfer fees, interest-bearing
  balances and issuer rebases are not modelled yet (B13 lifecycle
  adjustments); until then they surface as flows to acknowledge, never as
  silent adjustments.
- The instance attribution rule needs exactly one active instance of the
  same wallet and strategy; a second instance of the same strategy in the
  same wallet leaves new baskets at wallet level with the reason visible
  in the attribution, which a person must resolve.
- A sell of tokens the platform never bought consumes no lot and is
  reported as a shortfall in the worker log and the projection; the
  holdings answer shows the wallet quantity, not a lot.
- Receipts are private records with a public opt-in; nothing in B12
  publishes them anywhere, and a receipt for an intent still in flight
  records its current state (a later state gets its own receipt).
- Production receipts wait on the KMS signer (OD-22); the routes answer
  `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` there rather than issuing unsigned records.
