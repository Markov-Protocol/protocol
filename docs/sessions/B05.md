  decision records `exposureSource` so consumers can tell.
- `provider_verified` evidence has no producer; an IP-based country signal
  is not collected and would not be sufficient.

  them on submission and settlement.
- Beta caps (`BETA_*`) tighten every account once configured; the approved
  values are undecided (OD-12).
