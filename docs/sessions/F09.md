## Delivered

- One review for every trade: `/review/new` starts a review of exactly
  what the link named (a pinned strategy version by id, owner's copy or
  public projection; or one admitted instrument by canonical id), with
  one of the person's verified wallets and its balances observed by the
  API, an exact stablecoin budget in base units (prefilled from the
  builder's Activate stage), the budget mode, a slippage limit defaulting
  to the platform's 0.50% capped by the person's effective policy limit
  (tighten only), eligibility, local hints for the per-order cap (a
  basket's largest constituent share) and the observed balance, and one
  intent created with a per-visit idempotency key. Nothing is quoted or
  reserved on that page.
- `/review/[intentId]`: the plan the API builds for the exact budget,
  shown in full before any approval: input and allocation (largest
  remainder in base units, cash that stays, fee reserve), every
  constituent with weight, max input, expected and minimum output at the
  slippage limit, price impact, slippage and its transaction, every fee
  with its basis and the fee payer, signatures and transactions, atomic
  or staged semantics (the staged notice lists the transactions in order
  with the worst-case spend and the cash still in the wallet and needs
  its own acknowledgement), policy version, eligibility decision and
  per-constituent decisions with expiry, validity with a live countdown,
  quote source and references, funds observed, network, plan id and
  hash. Approval is recorded against the hash on screen; a refreshed
  plan is shown as a difference (binding fields only, per constituent)
  before approval can be enabled again; expired terms read as expired
  with a refresh, a replaced plan as superseded; a differing connected
  wallet or network, no wallet and a newer strategy version are called
  out; refusals become actions (insufficient stablecoin or SOL with both
  shortfalls, policy denial with codes, budget below the route minimum
  with the smallest workable budget, no output, unavailable quotes, rate
  limits, expired, plan changed) and never a bypass; cancel with
  confirmation; "Sign transaction 1 of N" unavailable with its reason
  until F10; `/review` lists the person's reviews. Another person's
  intent is "not found"; review pages are `noindex`.
- Entry points: the builder's Activate stage ("Review investment" for
  the newest frozen version with the wallet and budget carried over,
  unavailable until a version is frozen), the strategy page and version
  page (owner and public views), and the instrument page's Buy ("Review a
  buy" when the person can quote it; selling waits for F10).
- Plumbing: proxy allowlist for the seven B09 routes, contract-matrix
  rows, route registry, TanStack queries and mutations, a pure review
  model (`plan-model.ts`: validity, countdown, next action, leg and fee
  rows, plan difference, context warnings, refusal guidance).
- e2e API launcher: applies the effective fixture split so XSFXA is
  quoteable (policy keeps a constituent with an effective, unapplied
  corporate action unquoteable, as the startup check already exercised).
- Docs: frontend README, routes and journeys (route rows and the F09
  journeys; earlier "unavailable until F09" notes updated), contract map,
  verification (F09 section and two acceptance rows), security and
  privacy, backend `api.md` note, README.

## Provider verification status

No provider is involved. The venue is the fixture venue of B09
(synthetic deterministic quotes with 30-second validity through a program
id that exists on no network); every plan shown is IMPLEMENTED and
FIXTURE_VERIFIED. No quote was taken from a live venue (OD-21), nothing
was signed, submitted, reserved or spent, no message was sent. Fees on
screen are the API's bounds under the beta fee policy, not a fee market
observation.

## Unresolved risks and dependencies

- Signing and submission do not exist yet: the approval binds the person
  to a plan hash, and F10/B10 must check the same hash, intent, signer,
  chain and expiry again before any wallet popup and re-evaluate policy
  at submission with a reservation.
- The fixture venue's 30-second validity makes the expiry path easy to
  reach; a live venue's validity, route minimums and price impact are
  unknown until OD-21 is resolved, and the review will show whatever the
  API answers.
- Frozen legs are ordered by instrument id (B07), so a basket's
  transaction order follows that order, not the order the person
  assembled; the review shows it explicitly.
- Whole-basket simulation and composition (B11) are absent; a staged
  basket is a sequence of single-constituent transactions and the page
  says so.
- The sell direction, maintenance and assistant proposals will land on
  the same review (F10, F13, F14); the screen accepts only basket
  investments and single buys today.
- Screen-reader journeys through the review are not verified; keyboard
  approval is (Space on the acknowledgement, Enter on approve).
- At 320 px the top bar is crowded: the wallet chip and the connection
  badge now stay on one line each (the wallet name is hidden below the
  `md` breakpoint), but the shell's "Markov is ready" status still sits
  close to the chip on the second row; a shell-level layout for narrow
  widths belongs to a shell session.
