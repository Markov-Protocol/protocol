## Delivered

- `@markov/amounts` (pure): BigInt decimals with explicit rounding modes,
  exact and shortest conversion of on-chain doubles, raw ↔ scaled quantity
  conversion that reports the multiplier used and any information loss,
  and mirrors of the Token-2022 UI conversion helpers used only to measure
  their floating-point divergence.
- `@markov/contracts`: listed-stock fields (`underlying`), extension
  assessment, multiplier evidence and as-of resolution, the corporate-action
  feed contract v1 and records, lifecycle on every instrument, quantity
  conversion, snapshot kinds, `underlying_equity` reference prices.
- `@markov/catalog`: Token-2022 extension data parsers against the verified
  layouts and the V1 extension policy (supported / review_required /
  unsupported with plain-language findings, double rebasing always
  unsupported), corporate-action validation and planning (unknown products
  never create instruments; applied events are immutable to feeds),
  multiplier-after-action derivation, multiplier-as-of with completeness,
  lifecycle-aware availability (`ISSUER_HALTED`,
  `CORPORATE_ACTION_PENDING`, `MIGRATION_REQUIRED`, `INSTRUMENT_SUNSET`,
  `MULTIPLIER_UNKNOWN`), fixture encoders for extension data.
- `@markov/issuer-xstocks`: product and corporate-action fixture sources
  (four synthetic listed stocks with scaled, fee-bearing, delegated and
  paused mints; eight events including a split, halt/resume, migration,
  sunset, a future multiplier change, an unmatched and an invalid event)
  and bounded configured-URL sources; the live mapping is BLOCKED (OD-18).
- `@markov/db`: migration `0003_listed_stocks` (underlying and lifecycle
  columns, snapshot kind, verification compatibility, corporate actions,
  multiplier evidence with a unique point per source) and store functions
  with pending-status guards and batch reads.
- `apps/api`: verification now assesses extensions and records on-chain
  multiplier evidence (including a scheduled multiplier); admission refuses
  unsupported extension sets and requires `evidence.extensionReview` for
  review-required ones; corporate-action ingestion, application (effective
  time enforced; multiplier derived from the previous value or, when none
  exists, recorded from the on-chain value observed afterwards with its
  basis) and rejection; public corporate-action, multiplier history,
  multiplier-as-of and quantity-conversion routes; lifecycle on every
  instrument. Config: `XSTOCKS_FEED_URL`, `XSTOCKS_EVENTS_URL`.
- `apps/cli`: `catalog events ingest|list|apply|reject`, `catalog
  multiplier`, `catalog convert`.
- Fixture RPC now encodes extension data for both issuers through the
  built catalog package; the headless startup check runs the listed-stock
  journey (extension policy, refused admission of the fee-bearing mint,
  split application, exact conversion).
- Docs: `instrument-admission.md` and `accounting-methodology.md` (new),
  `catalog.md`, `api.md`, `provider-capabilities.md`, `open-decisions.md`
  (OD-18), `threat-model.md`, `operations.md`, `source-register.md`
  (SR-TOKEN-02, SR-XSTOCKS-01), `architecture.md`, README, `.env.example`;

## Limitations and unresolved risks

- No real xStocks mint was read; the extension policy is verified on
  synthetic bytes and the two recorded mainnet mints from B03 only.
- A split whose effective time precedes the first on-chain evidence is
  recorded from the observed on-chain value with an explicit basis; the
  period before the first evidence remains incomplete by design.
- Transfer-fee mints are unsupported in V1 rather than accounted; hook
  programs are not evaluated.
- Distributions are recorded as events without accounting effects (B12).
- Scheduled re-ingestion and holder notifications for halts, migrations and
  sunsets belong to B15 and B16.
