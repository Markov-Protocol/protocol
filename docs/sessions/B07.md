## Unresolved risks and dependencies

- Versions are immutable at the API and store level only; on-chain
  anchoring of `manifestHash` is B08. The recorded vectors must be
  reproduced by the Rust implementation before any registration.
- Admission snapshots are evidence at freeze time. A later delisting or
  pause leaves the version as it was; execution (B09/B10) must re-check
  availability and the policy at order time.
- Concentration is advisory in a recipe; the binding check is the
  execution policy's account-level exposure with real holdings (B05, B09).
- `ISSUER_CONCENTRATION` and `COMPANY_CONCENTRATION` use the policy
  ceilings from `ceilingLimits`; if the beta caps change, existing
  warnings are recomputed on the next draft read (they are not stored).
- Rate limits on freezes are per client address.
